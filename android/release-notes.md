FUJI Grocery Tracker native Android review build (Android 8.0+).

- Same 14,076 supplied items and prices as the FUJI website, joined by SKU to HYPERMART.
- Offline browsing and scanning, basket totals and budgets, private shelf prices, saved trips, lists, favorites, item photos and CSV sharing.
- Price date unavailable. Prices may not always be accurate. Estimate only. Final prices are confirmed at the cashier.
- No account, ads, analytics, paid services or internet permission. Personal shopping data stays on each device.

This is an Android debug-signed APK for review, not a Google Play release. Enable installation from your browser or file manager when Android asks, then open the APK. Debug build signatures can change between CI builds; do not assume an in-place update is possible across review builds.

Website: https://fuji-grocery-tracker-git-codex-fuji-shopper-hypermart-fuji-92d1.vercel.app/ (protected Vercel review preview).
